How this works

  1. Your question is mapped to a retailer and product.
  2. Approved, parameterised Cypher tools traverse FalkorDB across retailers, routes, stock, orders and deliveries.
  3. Rules separate observed facts from inferences and flag missing records.

No LLM-written Cypher is executed. Confidence is qualitative and rule-based, not a probability.
